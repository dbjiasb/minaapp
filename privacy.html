Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 24, 2026

At Charix AI, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how data flows through our AI features when you use Charix AI.

AI Data Flow Disclosure: Charix AI currently routes AI chat requests through developer-operated backend services and uses OpenAI services, including ChatGPT models, to generate AI responses. Your prompts, conversation content, and other necessary request data may therefore be transmitted to OpenAI for inference as part of providing the feature.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Info Sign-in data such as email address and third-party login credentials/tokens (e.g., Apple or Google sign-in).
  • Profile Info Nickname, avatar, bio, birthday, and other profile fields you edit.
  • User Content Prompts, chat messages, character settings, and other text you submit.
  • Media Content Images, audio, or videos you upload or create in app features.
  • Support Data Feedback, reports, and support communications.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & App Data Device identifier, app version, OS version, language, and diagnostic information.
  • Usage Data Feature usage, session events, and performance/security logs.
  • Attribution Data Advertising attribution identifiers (such as IDFA/GAID where available) and campaign metadata.

2. AI Model and Data Flow

When you use AI chat or generation features, the typical data flow is:

  • Step 1: Your input is sent from app client to Charix AI backend services over encrypted channels.
  • Step 2: The backend routes required fields to OpenAI services, including ChatGPT models, for AI inference and response generation.
  • Step 3: The generated result is returned to the app and may be stored in your account history for continuity.
  • Step 4: Limited logs may be retained for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.

For media upload, voice/video calls, ads attribution, or payments, required data may be processed by relevant infrastructure/payment providers only for delivering those functions.

We currently use OpenAI as an external AI inference provider for certain AI features. If we materially change AI model providers, add additional external inference providers, or materially change the categories of data shared for AI processing, we will update this Privacy Policy before rollout and request consent where required by law.

3. How We Use Information

  • Service Delivery: Account management, chat continuity, and AI response generation.
  • Safety and Integrity: Abuse detection, fraud prevention, and policy enforcement.
  • Operations: Troubleshooting, debugging, quality monitoring, and service reliability.
  • Personalization: Recommending features, characters, and content preferences.
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting legal obligations and handling lawful requests.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal chat content. We may share limited data only in the following cases:

  • Infrastructure Providers: Hosting, storage, content delivery, and communication services needed to operate Charix AI.
  • AI Service Providers: OpenAI, which provides ChatGPT and related model services used to process prompts and generate AI responses for supported features.
  • SDK/Service Integrations: Sign-in providers, attribution/analytics tools, real-time communication vendors, and payment channels used by the app.
  • Legal and Safety Needs: Where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect users and platform security.
  • Business Transfers: During merger, acquisition, financing, or asset transfer, subject to lawful safeguards.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law. You may request account deletion through app settings, and associated data will be deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable law and technical constraints.

6. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in regions where we or our service providers operate. Where required, we apply appropriate transfer safeguards under applicable data protection law.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your data, and to object to certain processing activities. You may also request review of AI-related processing impacts where required by law.

To exercise rights, contact us at the email listed below. We may verify your identity before processing requests.

8. Data Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted transmission, access control, and monitoring mechanisms. No method of transmission or storage is absolutely secure, and you are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

9. Children's Privacy

Charix AI is not intended for children under 13 (or a higher age where required by local law). If we learn that we collected personal data from a child without required authorization, we will take steps to delete it.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through in-app notice, website update, or other reasonable means before they become effective.
